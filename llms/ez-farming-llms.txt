# EZ Farming

> EZ Farming is an agriculture investment and farm-ownership platform that lets individuals start or co-own a working farm without doing the day-to-day work. Members buy land in the EZFarming farm estate, join a farmer cooperative to co-own a farm, buy produce directly from managed farms, or invest in agricultural projects. Backed by 500 Global.

Note: EZ Farming is a consumer-facing agriculture platform. As of this writing it publishes no developer/API surface (no OpenAPI, SDKs, or developer portal); this file was generated from the public marketing site.
